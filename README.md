<h1 align="center">🔥 Streakora</h1>

<p align="center">
  A clean, distraction-free, and highly customizable streak counter designed to help you stay accountable.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>✨ Core Features</h2>

<p>
  Your goals are personal, and your dashboard should reflect that. Customization isn't just about looks—it's about emotional resonance.
</p>

<h3>🎨 1. Fully Customizable Aesthetics</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Custom Color Palettes:</strong> Assign specific hex codes or gradients to individual cards.</li>
  <li><strong>Flexible Card Sizes:</strong> Resize your streak cards dynamically between Small, Medium, and Large.</li>
</ul>

<h3>✉️ 2. Future Messaging</h3>
<p>
  Beat the mid-streak slump by sending motivational messages to your future self that only unlock when you hit specific milestones.
</p>
<h3>🗓️ 3. Interactive Historical Calendar View</h3>
<p>
  A comprehensive, unified birds-eye view of your accountability journey.
</p>

<hr>

<p>Built with Swift and SwiftUI</p>
